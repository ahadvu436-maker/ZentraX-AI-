## 5. Compliance Checklist

### 5.1 Corporate & Financial
- [ ] Business registration and licenses current
- [ ] Bank accounts reconciled monthly
- [ ] Books closed monthly; annual financial statements prepared
- [ ] Segregation of duties (requester ≠ approver ≠ reconciler)
- [ ] Expense policy documented and followed
- [ ] Record retention policy defined (typical: 7 years; verify locally)

### 5.2 Tax
- [ ] Tax registrations complete (income, VAT/GST, payroll)
- [ ] Filings submitted on time; proofs stored
- [ ] Cross-border and digital-services tax obligations reviewed
- [ ] Contractor vs. employee classification verified

### 5.3 Data Privacy & AI Regulation
- [ ] Privacy regimes mapped (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, local laws)
- [ ] Data Processing Agreements with vendors
- [ ] Data retention & deletion procedures documented
- [ ] Applicable AI regulations reviewed (e.g., EU AI Act risk classification)
- [ ] Offline/local data storage handling reviewed for privacy compliance

### 5.4 Contracts & IP
- [ ] Customer contracts reviewed by counsel
- [ ] Open-source license obligations tracked
- [ ] Trademarks / domains registered and renewing
- [ ] Third-party model/data licenses verified for commercial use

### 5.5 Marketing & Consumer Protection
- [ ] Advertising claims substantiated
- [ ] Email consent and unsubscribe compliance (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, etc.)
- [ ] AI-generated content disclosure where required
- [ ] Campaigns from `marketing/campaigns_strategy.md` legally reviewed
